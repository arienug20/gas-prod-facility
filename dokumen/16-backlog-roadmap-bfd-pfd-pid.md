## 1. Cara menggunakan backlog

Status `existing` berarti kemampuan/proses teramati dalam repository atau laporan fase 1; `partial` berarti ada prototipe dengan batas yang diketahui; `proposed` berarti pekerjaan belum dibuktikan implementasinya. Status bukan tingkat kesiapan desain. Owner ditulis sebagai peran sampai penunjukan aktual dilakukan. Dependency menunjukkan urutan logis; pekerjaan inventaris, pertanyaan data, dan review standar dapat berjalan paralel bila tidak memerlukan keputusan yang belum tersedia.

Kriteria acceptance harus mencakup kasus positif dan negatif yang disebutkan. Evidence mencatat artefak, output/tool version, fixture, hasil aktual, reviewer, dan keterbatasan. Jangan mengubah acceptance menjadi PASS hanya karena artefak dibuat. Jangan menambahkan nilai dummy ke basis GPF. Untuk pilot separator/scrubber, gunakan fixture sintetik berorientasi V-101 dengan scope test-only yang disetujui; jangan mengubah `dokumen/01-design-basis.md`. Pump dan flare menjadi regression fixture terpisah.

## 2. Kontrak umum dan data-first

Core engine memproses graph, status, rules, provenance dan issue bundle. Project profile memuat title block, sheet, legend, konvensi tag, standar yang diadopsi dan aturan client/company. Data GPF tetap berasal dari design basis dan sumber proyek terkendali. Ketiga lapisan dihubungkan adapter berversi; perubahan profile atau project data tidak boleh mengubah semantik core secara diam-diam.

Setiap nilai memiliki tipe/unit bila relevan, source ID/revisi/lokasi, `certainty_kind` (`source_fact`, `assumption`, `tbd`) dan `confirmation_state` (alur provenance/konfirmasi yang dijabarkan di §3.2), owner/reviewer, waktu review dan history. Kedua field tidak saling menyiratkan: fakta di sumber belum tentu terkonfirmasi, dan `confirmed` tidak sama dengan document approval. `assumption` wajib memiliki `authorization_ref` untuk otorisasi eksplisit; `tbd` harus bernilai null dan tidak dapat dikonfirmasi sebagai nilai tertentu. Simpan decision engineering terpisah dari status data dan approval dokumen. Unknown/null tidak sama dengan nol, default, `not applicable`, atau pass. Tag persistent hanya dibuat dari aturan penomoran/alokasi yang telah disetujui dan keputusan pemilik; belum ada alokasi berarti pertanyaan/hold, bukan auto-tag proyek.

Data-first workflow: (a) inventaris dokumen dan ekstrak item/sumber; (b) bandingkan nilai, satuan, basis operasi, scope dan revisi; (c) keluarkan pertanyaan primer adaptif yang mengunci dependency; (d) minta konfirmasi basis sebelum desain; (e) rekam keputusan eksplisit atas asumsi atau partial/TBD; (f) hitung ulang readiness per deliverable dan fase. Silence is not approval. BFD/PFD/P&ID dapat dibuat bertahap hanya atas otorisasi scope tertentu, dengan status preliminary/review dan holds terbuka yang terlihat.

## 3.2 Data request/questionnaire dan konfirmasi basis (BPD-002, 202, 203, 401, 402)

Questionnaire adalah elicitation engineering yang mengungkap keputusan dan sumber yang belum ada. Pertanyaan tidak boleh menyarankan default desain lewat contoh angka. Bentuk kanal dapat berupa percakapan yang diorkestrasi; UI/CLI khusus bukan prasyarat. Tanyakan secara adaptif berdasarkan applicability dan jawaban terdahulu, bukan mengulang pertanyaan yang sudah memiliki konfirmasi valid untuk scope yang sama.

### Intake umum dan state records

| Record/state | Perilaku wajib | Transition/evidence |
|---|---|---|
| `SourceRef` | Catat dokumen/artefak, ID/revisi/tanggal jika diketahui, lokasi halaman/section, pemilik asal, metode ekstraksi, dan hash bila tersedia. | `source → extracted` mencatat field hasil kutip/ekstraksi dan locator; ekstraksi tidak sama dengan konfirmasi. |
| `BasisItem` | Field bertipe dengan unit/basis bila relevan, applicability scope, source refs, `certainty_kind`, `confirmation_state`, optional `authorization_ref`, owner dan history. | `confirmation_state: source → extracted → unconfirmed → confirmed` hanya untuk nilai non-TBD setelah bukti/source dan konfirmasi eksplisit oleh role berwenang untuk scope/issue. `certainty_kind: source_fact` sendiri tidak membuat state confirmed/approved. |
| `Conflict` | Simpan semua nilai/klaim beserta provenance, scope dan alasan inkompatibel. | Conflict dapat diterapkan pada source claim atau basis yang sebelumnya confirmed bila bukti baru bertentangan; state conflict tidak menghapus nilai/source terdahulu. Tidak ada precedence otomatis selain hierarki sumber yang disetujui dan applicable. |
| `Superseded` | Sumber/record lama tetap terbaca dan linked ke pengganti; jangan hapus history. | State superseded hanya dengan source/decision pengganti dan dampak perubahan tercatat; provenance record lama tetap disimpan. |
| `Decision` | Opsi, applicability, rationale, alternatives/rejected reasons, owner/authority, evidence, affected objects/deliverables, status terbuka/terkonfirmasi. | Keputusan bukan perubahan certainty sumber atau approval dokumen; update ketiganya pada record terpisah. |
| `Approval` | Tujuan issue, deliverable/revisi, author/checker/approver role dan waktu/identitas yang benar-benar diberikan. | Persetujuan eksplisit scoped; tidak diwariskan otomatis ke revisi, tujuan issue, atau dokumen lain. |

**Aturan konfirmasi:** untuk pekerjaan rutin yang sudah berada dalam scope dan mempunyai basis/otorisasi aktif, konfirmasi yang masih berlaku tidak ditanyakan ulang. Tanyakan ulang bila sumber/revisi berubah, ada konflik baru, scope/issue berubah, atau field mempengaruhi keputusan baru. Jika jawaban tidak tersedia, lanjutkan inventaris dan tugas independen; jangan tandai setuju. Asumsi/partial/TBD membutuhkan otorisasi eksplisit dari user/role yang berwenang, dengan deliverable, scope, batas pemakaian, expiry/trigger review dan owner closure dicatat. Persetujuan contoh sintetik untuk pilot yang sudah diberikan hanya berlaku pada fixture test-only yang disepakati; tidak mengesahkan angka/tag sebagai data GPF.

## 3.3 Kontrak interface data awal (BPD-201–206)

Contoh/kontrak di sini **proposal**, belum berarti schema atau adapter sudah mendukungnya. Jangan mengubah JSON/CSV usulan menjadi API implemented tanpa versioning dan review. Kanal tanya-jawab boleh conversational; compiler/schema menerima jawaban eksplisit sebagai records.

### CSV H&MB minimal yang diusulkan

Header mapping harus disimpan, bukan diasumsikan dari urutan kolom. Setiap row mengidentifikasi `schema_version`, `source_id`, `source_revision`, `stream_id`, `field`, `value`, `unit`, `basis`, `case_id`, `phase/status`, `locator`, `review_state`. Metadata file menyimpan encoding/delimiter, origin, issued/reviewed state, import timestamp dan checksum. Nama kolom supplier boleh dipetakan adapter hanya dengan mapping yang direview. Tiap nilai tetap traceable ke sumber asli.

**Kategori error import/validation** (struktural): `schema_unsupported`, `required_field_missing`, `duplicate_id`, `bad_reference`, `invalid_type_or_unit`, `malformed_csv_json`, `unsupported_column_or_mapping`, `source_revision_missing`. Output berupa row/field locator dan remediation; importer menolak row/file sesuai kebijakan, tidak menghasilkan nilai hasil tebak.

**Kategori finding engineering** (semantik/status): `data_unconfirmed`, `source_conflict`, `mandatory_unknown`, `unit_basis_incomparable`, `applicability_unresolved`, `decision_open`, `study_evidence_missing`, `interface_mismatch`. Kategori ini bukan exception parsing. Unknown menghasilkan open finding/hold pada deliverable yang bergantung, bukan lulus atau input validator gagal.

## 3.4 Standar, simbol, legend dan signal audit (BPD-101–104)

## 3.10 Evidence and human responsibility per subtask

Every subtask record stores: subtask ID; parent BPD ID; input artifact/source revisions; output/schema/profile/rule versions; exact scenario IDs and expected/actual results; logs/backend identity; linked questions/decisions/holds; owner role; checker role/result; residual limitations; and hashes for released artifacts. Do not store confidential credentials or unrelated commercial data. A passing structural test does not mark engineering certainty or approval as confirmed.

Owner prepares and resolves the work within assigned discipline authority. Checker independently verifies the listed evidence and scope. Approval role is only the one designated by project procedure and appears on issue records only after an actual decision. For safety functions, process safety/instrument authority reviews C&E/SRS/SIS content; a software test does not approve it. For standard interpretation, designated standards focal and discipline checker record source and interpretation. For visual checks, reviewer is independent of drawing author as required by the workflow.

## 4. Readiness matrix minimum

Readiness reports use `ready for stated purpose`, `ready with approved holds`, `not ready`, or `not assessed`, with separate dimensions for data certainty, engineering decisions, model integrity, drafting, and document approval. A single green score is prohibited. `Ready with approved holds` only applies when a named role has explicitly authorized the hold and issue purpose; silence is not approval.

| Deliverable/fase | Minimum applicable evidence to evaluate | Not required blanket for every early issue | Typical blocker / hold |
|---|---|---|---|
| Design basis | Scope/battery limits, source hierarchy/revisions, units/basis, confirmed vs assumed/TBD data, operating/design envelope where applicable, system boundaries and unresolved questions; explicit basis confirmation for the requested work | Full equipment detailed design, final vendor dimensions or all discipline calculations unrelated to scope | Contradictory source hierarchy, missing primary data required for the next decision, unapproved assumption. |
| BFD | System purpose/boundary, major operations, stream identities/directions, source/provenance and applicable operation cases; unknowns/holds visible | Detailed P&ID valves, loop tags, nozzle schedule, final line specs | Missing source/destination or applicable operation needed to define flow/function. |
| PFD/H&MB | Unit operations, stream/component identities, phase and applicable flow/condition basis with units, operating cases, mass/energy balance status and reviewed calculation/import provenance | Final construction valve detail, universal relief or equipment calculations where not applicable | Inconsistent stream basis, unverified values used as fact, unresolved process boundary/critical case. |
| P&ID FEED | Confirmed scope/function/battery limits for intended review; applicable equipment, main lines, preliminary controls/protection and vendor/package boundaries; relevant startup/shutdown/upset/maintenance considerations; applicable studies/data status and owner-assigned holds | Every DED nozzle, final commodity/specification detail, final vendor dimensions and every unrelated calculation | Omitted applicable function; unknown without owner; unreviewed safety function; false implication that preliminary content is approved. |
| P&ID DED | Applicable approved basis; reconciled equipment/line/instrument/tag references; connections/nozzles and valve details required for requested release; loop/signal/C&E references, states/setpoints only where approved; drains/vents/tie-ins/utilities/operation detail as applicable; vendor/study closeout or explicitly approved hold per issue purpose | Detail demonstrably outside the drawing's boundary/contractual scope, with interface documented | Mandatory applicable safety/process data unresolved; inconsistent referenced documents; unapproved tag/design detail; incomplete review/approval. |

Readiness items are defined through WP1–WP7 and relevant project procedure. A gate checks applicability and evidence; it does not assert that the listed topics are all required in every system. FEED can carry preliminary or TBD information only when explicitly authorized, clearly status-marked, and not used to imply unresolved design is approved.

## 5. Standards review matrix workflow

For every standard/rule, preserve: (1) citation and exact edition/addenda from controlled source; (2) contract/adoption basis; (3) deliverables/symbols/decision applicability; (4) manual versus automated check; (5) interpretation and project legend mapping; (6) reviewer, date, evidence and open questions; (7) rule version and change impact. Automatic checks should verify only representable assertions, such as consistency with an approved project legend; clause interpretation and engineering adequacy remain manual unless a validated, bounded rule is explicitly accepted.

Initial subjects listed in roadmap §4 are candidates, not a declaration of compliance. If authorized copies are unavailable, mark individual entries `NOT ASSESSED`. PIP PIC001 is conditional on adoption. ISA 5.4/IEC 62424 are later interface candidates, not a replacement for P&ID review. IEC/ISA 61511 involvement does not mean the tool performs the safety lifecycle. API/ASME is selected by actual scope, not applied as a blanket bundle.

## 6. Minimum interface contract and change impact

Schema/API definitions must be versioned and shipped with compatibility adapters. Initial records should cover `SourceRef`, `BasisItem`, `Decision`, `Hold`, `Object`, `Port`, `Connection`, `FunctionApplicability`, `InstrumentLoop/Signal`, `StudyRef`, `DocumentIssue`, and `Finding`. Each record needs stable ID, source/version, status, owner role, creation/update history, and typed references; numeric fields carry unit and basis. Rendering/profile-only presentation fields must not overwrite process semantics. Store output schema version and producer version in every serialized artifact.

Adapters accept old phase1 model/manifest/schedules as explicit legacy input, validate, and report what cannot be represented. They must not infer internal valve ports, physical nozzles, process service, or project approval from geometry. A schema migration produces a report and preserves source artifacts. Breaking changes require a new version and migration tests.

Change-impact propagation should follow source→basis item→stream/equipment/function/decision→graph/document/register/calculation/review/issue. Report direct and derived effects, rules and provenance; require discipline owner acknowledgement where meaning changes. A changed tag allocation, port topology, signal/action, pressure basis, source revision, standard/profile version or issue purpose triggers appropriate checks/review. Position-only edits still trigger drafting QA but do not erase semantic delta history.

## 7. Gate and evidence policy

Evidence bundle per completed task: task ID, input source/revisions, schema/rules/profile/core versions, test fixture scope, exact command/backend, positive and negative test outputs, visual evidence where drawing changes, reviewer role/result, known gaps, linked decisions/holds, and output hashes. Tests are not run merely to populate this proposed backlog; they are required as implementation acceptance evidence once the work is authorized. Historical results are cited with original date/scope.

For a drawing task, the local discipline in AGENTS.md applies: load `company-cad-standard` plus patched `dxf`; confirm company override exists; build → `cad/apply_text.py` (MTEXT and pens) → validate 0 errors → snapshot → independent visual check. Do not use outlined text for engineering drawing. Bump drawing revision/title block/revision table and record change description. Report residual issues and actual CAD backend; an API stub is not proof of external engine validation.
